## What it does

Normally the logo on the lid follows the screen brightness and goes dark when the lid is closed. Glow My Logo keeps it lit at a brightness you set.

- **Your brightness, fixed.** Set the logo brightness yourself with a slider in the menu bar. It stays there however bright or dim the screen is. Only the logo changes; the display stays as it is.
- **Glows with the lid closed.** Keeps the logo lit in clamshell mode while an external display is connected.
- **Gentle on the eyes.** The logo fades in and out smoothly, and turns off when your Mac goes to sleep.
- **Stays out of the way.** Lives in the menu bar, opens at login and keeps itself up to date.

## Requirements

- MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR display (14-inch or 16-inch, 2026)
- macOS 27.2 or later
- Earlier MacBook Pro models are not supported. Their logo has no light to control.

## Install

1. Download the latest `GlowMyLogo-x.y.z.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/oh-my-mintchoco/glow-my-logo-releases/releases/latest).
2. Unzip it and move **GlowMyLogo** to your Applications folder.
3. Open it. The icon appears in the menu bar; drag the slider to set the logo brightness.

The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. It updates itself.

## Questions

**Is it free?** Yes. It is free to download and use, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

**Is it safe for my Mac?** The app never drives the logo beyond the maximum brightness calibrated for your Mac at the factory, and it needs no administrator rights.

**Does it collect any data?** No. The only network request it makes is the check for updates.

**How do I remove it?** Quit the app and move it to the Trash. Quitting hands the logo back to macOS.

More answers at [glowmylogo.app](https://glowmylogo.app/#questions).
